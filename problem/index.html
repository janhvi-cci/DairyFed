The core problem

Where the System Breaks Down

India's dairy ecosystem is physically connected, but information about that milk is fragmented across registers, phone calls, circulars and memory.

  • Limited visibility into milk quality reports
  • No easy way to track payments
  • Minimal awareness of government schemes
  • Difficulty accessing veterinary services
  • Manual records and delayed feedback
  • Circulars shared through fragmented channels
  • Complaint tracking is manual
  • Report preparation consumes staff time
  • Multiple manual registers
  • Delayed reporting upward
  • Fragmented information across districts
  • Slow feedback loops from the field
  • No real-time decision support
  • Limited operational visibility
  • Difficulty consolidating data
